The Vision

The personal exposure passport

Your health has a history beyond your medical records.

Every day, we encounter a complex range of environmental, dietary, occupational, and lifestyle influences. Many are invisible, some are measurable, and others are only beginning to be understood.

Pereluno envisions a way to understand and document these influences throughout life. One evolving personal record would connect information across 15 interconnected domains.

A New Perspective on Personal Health

Beyond what happens inside the body.

Traditional health records tell one part of the story. Pereluno looks at the environmental and everyday influences surrounding our lives—from the air we breathe to the places we inhabit.

Our ambition is to make that story more accessible, structured, and meaningful.

Designed to Evolve

Built for a Changing World

Our environments are evolving. So are the materials, technologies, products, and processes we encounter in everyday life.

Pereluno envisions an adaptable framework designed to incorporate emerging exposure categories, advancing scientific evidence, and future measurement technologies.

A personal exposure record designed to evolve with the world around us.
